Why this room matters for Leland

Leland's marketplace needs two things: experts who coach and ambitious people who buy coaching. This dinner seats both, three weeks before internship recruiting opens.

Your next coaches

Leland lists school admissions, consulting, and finance careers among its core coaching categories. The people who deliver that coaching look like this room: recent MBB, bulge-bracket, and PE alumni who just went through the admissions cycle themselves.

    Your next customers

    Every MBA1 in the room recruits for a summer 2027 internship starting in January, and most are switching function, industry, or geography. Coaching demand peaks in the twelve weeks after this dinner.
